Before you start

Thanks for testing soglu. It’s an early, unreleased prototype, so we ask everyone to agree to three things first.

  1. Keep it confidential. Please don’t share the link, password, screenshots, recordings or details of soglu with anyone outside the soglu team.
  2. Your notes come to us. Each note you send goes to the soglu team with your name and email, so we can follow up. Other testers never see it.
  3. It’s a prototype. The people, places and listings are made up, and features may change or never ship.

We only use your name and email for this beta, and for soglu updates if you tick the box.
